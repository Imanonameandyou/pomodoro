I

The brand

What Daje Pinsa is, what the name means, and the ideas every asset has to carry.

The meaning behind Daje

Daje (pronounced DAH-yeh) is Roman slang — a shout of encouragement that lands somewhere between come on, let's go, and you got this. It carries energy, warmth, and celebration. The name is a promise about the feeling at the table, not a description of the food.

Always spell it Daje Pinsa — never "Daje's," never "Daje Pizza." Include the pronunciation on first use in any consumer-facing piece where the audience is new to the brand.

Positioning

Roman pinsa — the lighter, older, crispier ancestor of pizza — brought to Orlando by an Italian restaurateur who trained in Rome. The dough is a signature flour blend, fermented 72 hours, and the result is airy and highly digestible rather than heavy. The brand sits at the intersection of authentic and light, and both halves must be present.

Clear space

Minimum clear space = the height of the "D" on all four sides

Usage

Do

  • Use the full lockup — wordmark plus oval — wherever space allows
  • Place on white, cream, or a dark photographic area with enough contrast
  • Use the oval mark alone only as a favicon, app icon, or small social avatar
  • Scale proportionally, always

Don't

  • Recolor the wordmark to anything but terracotta or solid white
  • Add a drop shadow, outline, gradient, or bevel
  • Rotate, skew, condense, or re-space the letterforms
  • Set it over a busy area of a photo without a scrim behind it
  • Rebuild the wordmark in Cinzel or any other typeface
III

Color

Terracotta leads, teal supports, warm cream carries everything. Click any swatch to copy its hex.

Primary — terracotta

The brand's signature. Used for headlines, primary buttons, the logo, and accent rules. Never let a second saturated color compete with it on the same screen.

Secondary — Roman teal

A supporting accent only: confirmations, informational tags, secondary highlights. It should never headline a layout or replace terracotta on a call to action.

Surfaces & neutrals

Cream is the default page background — not white. White is reserved for cards and navigation so content lifts off the page.

Proportion

RoleShareApplied to
Warm cream + white~70%Page and card backgrounds, negative space
Terracotta~20%Headlines, buttons, logo, dividers, announcement bar
Charcoal / grey text~8%Body copy, captions, footer
Roman teal~2%Confirmations, tags, occasional accents

Contrast rules

Terracotta on white and on cream both clear AA for body text. White on terracotta clears AA. Roman teal on white does not clear AA at body sizes — use Teal Text #177B82 for any teal-colored text. Never set terracotta text on teal, or teal text on terracotta.

Rules

Do

  • Set Cinzel in uppercase with generous tracking — it was designed from carved Roman capitals
  • Keep Cinzel headlines to one or two lines
  • Use Marcellus when a heading needs warmth rather than authority
  • Set body copy at 1.6–1.7 line height, max ~70 characters per line

Don't

  • Set Cinzel in sentence case or at default tracking
  • Use Cinzel for body copy or anything under 14px
  • Introduce a fourth typeface
  • Stack Cinzel and Marcellus headings directly on top of each other — put an eyebrow or rule between

Fallback stacks: Cinzel and Marcellus fall back to Georgia, serif. Satoshi falls back to Helvetica Neue, Arial, sans-serif.

Voice

Warm, proud, and specific. A Roman host explaining something they love — never a restaurant selling you dinner.

Attributes

We areWe are not
Warm and welcomingCasual to the point of sloppy
Proud of the traditionSnobbish or gatekeeping
Specific about craftBuried in technical jargon
ConfidentBoastful or hype-driven
Celebratory — daje!Loud for the sake of loud

Core lines

  • Born in Rome, served in Orlando.Primary tagline. Use as the hero headline and in paid social.
  • Not just pizza. Pinsa.The category-education line. Leads any explainer about what pinsa is.
  • Authentic Roman pinsa · Rome's lighter take on pizzaDescriptor subhead. Pairs under the primary tagline.
  • Born in Italy. Shared with Orlando.Heritage and community angle. Use on story and about pages.
  • A Roman tradition, served in Orlando.Product-page and menu framing.
  • Bring the taste of Italy to every gatheringCatering and events only.

Writing rules

Do

  • Lead with a concrete detail — 72 hours, imported from Italy, signature flour blend
  • Use short declaratives. Periods, not exclamation points
  • Say "light" and "digestible," and let the reader draw the health conclusion
  • Use Italian sparingly and always in context: daje, pinsa, Romana
  • Write CTAs as plain actions: Order now. Read our story. Join the list.

Don't

  • Make medical, nutritional, or weight-loss claims
  • Call pinsa a "pizza alternative" — it's its own thing, older than pizza
  • Stack Italian words for flavor when English is clearer
  • Use urgency gimmicks, countdowns, or all-caps shouting in body copy
  • Describe the food as "artisanal," "elevated," or "curated"
VI

Photography

Warm, natural, close. The food should look like it was just set down in front of you.

Direction

AttributeDirection
LightWarm natural daylight, soft shadows. No cool or clinical white balance
SurfacesWood, stone, linen, terracotta. Never a seamless white studio sweep
FramingOverhead or low three-quarter angle, close enough to read the crust's texture
CrustAlways show the airy, blistered edge — it is the product proof
PeopleGroups sharing at the table, mid-conversation. Candid, not posed to camera
Color gradeWarm and slightly rich. No heavy filters, HDR, or crushed blacks

Subject priority

In order: the pinsa itself and its crust · the table and the people around it · ingredients and process · the room. Portraits of Chef Gabriele are shot in-restaurant, in branded polo, natural light, relaxed.

Text over images

Set headlines in white Cinzel uppercase over a dark scrim at 40–60% opacity — never terracotta text on a photograph. Keep the crop clear behind the type; if the image is busy, move the type into a solid band.

VII

Components

The repeating pieces of the site, built from the tokens above.

Buttons

Fully rounded pills, uppercase Satoshi Bold at 13px, +0.10em tracking. Primary terracotta for the main action, outlined for secondary, teal reserved for signup and confirmation actions. One primary button per view.

Section lockup

The site's core layout unit: a small tracked eyebrow, a Cinzel headline, centered body copy, one button. Reuse it rather than inventing new section shapes.

Our heritage

Born in Italy·Shared with Orlando

We grew up around tables where meals lasted for hours, conversations mattered, and everyone was treated like family. That's the feeling we hope to bring to Orlando every day.

Announcement bar

Opening fall 2026 · Follow our journey on Instagram @dajepinsa

Full-bleed terracotta, white uppercase Satoshi at 11px, +0.18em tracking. Sits above the navigation. One message at a time — never rotate more than two.

Cards

White or off-white fill, 1px Clay Light #F3C4BE border, no border radius, no drop shadow. Content stack: Marcellus kicker, Cinzel uppercase title in terracotta, Satoshi body in muted grey. The three pillars in section I are the reference build.
